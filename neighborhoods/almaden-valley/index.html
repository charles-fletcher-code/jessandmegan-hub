Almaden Valley

San Jose's most sought-after hillside community

Quick Answer

Almaden Valley is a quiet, upscale neighborhood in southwest San Jose known for top-rated schools (Almaden Elementary, Bret Harte Middle, Leland High), abundant trails, and a village-like feel. Most homes are single-family and range from the mid-$1M to $3M+. It's ideal for families who want Silicon Valley access without urban density.
